How It Works

A simple order-based model connecting users and Relayers.

STEP 01

Create Order

Choose the source chain, destination chain, asset and amount, then create a swap order.

STEP 02

Relayer Takes Order

Independent Relayers browse open orders and select orders they want to execute.

STEP 03

Pre-Fund

The Relayer uses its own inventory to provide the requested asset on the destination chain.

STEP 04

Settlement

After the source transaction is confirmed, the Relayer receives the source asset and reward.
